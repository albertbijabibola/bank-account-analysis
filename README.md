# Bank Account Activity and Customer Retention Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes bank account data to identify patterns associated
with account activity, inactivity, and closure.

The analysis focuses on account status, account type, branch,
account balances, account tenure, and account opening dates.

The objective is to generate evidence-based insights that could help
a bank better understand account activity and identify areas that
may require further investigation.

## Business Problem

Banks need to understand how their customer accounts behave over time.
A high number of inactive or closed accounts may indicate areas that
require further investigation.

This project uses exploratory data analysis and statistical analysis
to examine patterns associated with account status.

The analysis does not assume that a particular factor causes account
closure. Instead, relationships observed in the data are treated as
patterns that require further investigation.

## Objectives

- Examine the distribution of account status.
- Compare active, inactive, and closed accounts.
- Analyze account status across account types and branches.
- Examine account balances by account status.
- Analyze account tenure and account opening dates.
- Identify unusual or notable patterns in the data.
- Develop evidence-based findings and recommendations.
- Identify limitations and additional data required for deeper analysis.

## Key Business Questions

1. What proportion of accounts are active, inactive, or closed?
2. Which account types have the highest number of inactive or closed accounts?
3. How does account status vary across branches?
4. How do account balances differ by account status?
5. Does account tenure differ between account-status groups?
6. Are there periods with unusually high account closures?
7. What patterns should the bank investigate further?

## Dataset

The dataset contains bank account information used for analytical

The dataset will be examined for:

- Missing values

- Invalid dates
- Incorrect data types
- Unusual values

## Limitations

The analysis can identify relationships and patterns in the available
data, but it may not establish the actual reasons why customers become
inactive or close their accounts.

Additional information such as customer feedback, transaction activity,
income information, product usage, complaints, and customer interaction
history could provide further insight.
