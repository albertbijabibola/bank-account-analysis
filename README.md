# Bank Account Activity and Customer Retention Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes bank account data to identify patterns associated
with account activity, inactivity, and closure.

The analysis focuses on account status, account type, branch,
account balances, account tenure, and account opening dates.

The goal is to generate evidence-based insights that can help a bank
better understand account activity and identify areas that may require
further investigation.

## Business Problem

A bank wants to better understand patterns associated with active,
inactive, and closed customer accounts.

Understanding these patterns can help the bank identify areas that
require further investigation and support data-driven decisions
around customer account management and retention.

This analysis does not assume that a particular factor causes account
closure. Instead, relationships and patterns observed in the data
will be investigated and interpreted based on the available evidence.

## Objectives

- Examine the distribution of account status.
- Compare active, inactive, and closed accounts.
- Analyze account status across account types.
- Compare account activity across branches.
- Examine account balances by account status.
- Analyze account tenure.
- Examine account opening trends over time.
- Identify notable patterns and potential areas for further investigation.
- Provide evidence-based recommendations.

## Key Business Questions

1. What proportion of accounts are active, inactive, or closed?
2. Which account types have the highest proportion of inactive or closed accounts?
3. How does account status vary across branches?
4. How do account balances differ by account status?
5. Does account tenure differ between active, inactive, and closed accounts?
6. Are there periods with unusually high account closures?
7. What patterns should the bank investigate further?

## Limitations

The analysis can identify relationships and patterns within the
available data, but it may not establish the actual reasons why
customers become inactive or close their accounts.

Additional information such as transaction activity, customer
feedback, product usage, complaints, and customer interaction history
could provide deeper insight.
